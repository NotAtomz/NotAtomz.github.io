Assists & Helpers
Neighbor Highlight
Hover a number to glow its unrevealed neighbors — shows which cells it could be counting.
Safe Cell Hint CHEAT
Hover a satisfied number (flags = count) to show its remaining neighbors in green — they're safe to click.
Danger Hint CHEAT
If unflagged neighbors = remaining mines, hover highlights them red — they're all mines.
Auto-chord on Hover
Middle-click (or double-tap) a revealed number whose flag count matches to clear its safe neighbors automatically.
Flag Counter Badge
Show a small badge on revealed numbers indicating how many more flags are needed around them.
First-click Safety
Your first click is always safe — mines are placed after you click.
Question Mark Cycle
Right-click cycles: unflagged → 🚩 flag → ❓ question → unflagged. Useful for marking uncertain cells.
