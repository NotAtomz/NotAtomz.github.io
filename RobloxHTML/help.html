Blox Emulation Help

Basic instructions for playing and loading your own maps.

How to Play

After you load a map and enter the emulator, use the standard movement controls to move around the world.

  • W, A, S, D — Move your character.
  • Space — Jump.
  • Mouse — Look around and control the camera.
  • Right Mouse Button — Orbit the camera while in third person.
  • Mouse Wheel — Zoom the camera in and out.

The emulator supports classic Roblox-style parts, physics, collisions, touch events, humanoids, seats, joints, scripts, and other supported objects included in the uploaded map.

How to Upload Maps

Maps are loaded as exported .lua or .luau files. Build your map inside Roblox Studio first, then use the Blox Emulation Workspace Exporter plugin to convert the contents of Workspace into a script that the browser emulator can load.

  • Open your place in Roblox Studio.
  • Keep the objects you want to export inside Workspace.
  • Run the Blox Emulation Workspace Exporter plugin.
  • The plugin creates ServerStorage.BloxEmulationExport.
  • Open that ModuleScript and copy its generated source into a .lua or .luau file.
  • Return to the Blox Emulation home page.
  • Click Choose File and select the exported map file.
  • Click Enter to load the map into the emulator.

Scripts inside supported exported objects are included after the map is created, so map logic such as touch events and moving parts can run once the world finishes loading.
